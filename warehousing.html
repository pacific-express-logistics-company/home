Secure storage and smart distribution, connected to your freight.

Pacific Express warehousing links storage directly to your road, air and sea movements, so goods are received, stored and dispatched without gaps.

What you get

Key capabilities of our warehousing service.

Secure storage

Safe, organised storage for pallets, cartons and containers.

Inventory management

Know what is in stock and where it is.

Pick, pack & dispatch

A simple process from first contact to delivery.

Receive

Store
